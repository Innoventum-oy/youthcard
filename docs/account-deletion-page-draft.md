# Delete your Youth Card account and data

You can request deletion of your Youth Card account in the app. Sign in, open **My Card**, scroll to **Account deletion**, tap **Delete account**, and confirm. You will be signed out when the request succeeds.

If you cannot sign in, email **info@youthcard.eu** with the subject **Youth Card account deletion**. Send the request from the email address linked to your account, or tell us the phone number and region linked to it so we can verify the request. Do not send your password.

Deleting your account removes your account and the personal information connected to it, including your profile and contact details, card details, and identifiable visit records. Anonymous statistical information about visits may remain for reporting after it has been separated from your identity. **[Confirm whether anonymous statistics have a fixed retention period; state that period here, or say that no fixed expiry applies.]**

If we must retain any identifiable information for a legal reason, we will tell you what is retained, why, and for how long when responding to your request. **[Confirm whether such retention occurs and replace this sentence with the actual categories and periods, or state that none is retained.]**

For questions about account or data deletion, contact **info@youthcard.eu**.
