# Youth Card app privacy policy

This page explains how information is used in the Youth Card app. The data controller is Innoventum Oy, represented by Janne Leinonen, at Juhonkuja 7, 80260 Joensuu, Finland. For privacy questions, contact [info@youthcard.eu](mailto:info@youthcard.eu).

We use your consent to process account, registration and visit data. Innoventum Oy hosts the service. When you participate in an activity, the participating municipality can access your full profile information to organise the activity and record your visit. Each municipality is responsible for the account data it handles. Active-account data is not transferred internationally. Account data is removed after a full year of inactivity.

## Information used by the app

When you create and use an account, the service uses the details you provide for that account, such as your name and contact details. It uses your account information to display your card, let you sign in, and provide the activities and benefits available to you. If you provide your age or gender, these details may be used in activity-visit statistics. The service also records information about your registrations and visits to activities so that those features work.

If you choose to add a profile picture, attachments to a form, or feedback, the app uses the content you provide for those features. The app can use your location to show distances to activities and when recording a visit if you grant location access. It can use your camera to scan QR codes or take a picture when you choose those features.

## Account deletion and retained statistics

You can permanently delete your account through **My Card → Account Deletion** in the app, or request deletion by email. See [Delete your Youth Card account](https://youthcard.eu/en/account-deletion) for the steps.

Deletion removes your account and all information that identifies you. Statistical records of activity visits remain and may include age and gender if you provided them. Those records are no longer linked to your account or other identifying account information. These remaining statistical records have no fixed expiry. You cannot currently delete individual account data while keeping your account.

## Questions and requests

Contact [info@youthcard.eu](mailto:info@youthcard.eu) if you have a question about your information, want to withdraw consent, exercise your rights to access, correct or erase your data, or request account deletion without using the app. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman. Do not send your password by email.
