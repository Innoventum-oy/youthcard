# Youth Card

Youth Card helps young people discover activities in their area and take part in them. Browse activities and events, register where registration is available, and use your personal card in the app.

Your card includes a QR code. The app can also scan QR codes to record visits to activities. Available benefits and activities depend on your area.

Need help? Visit [Youth Card support](https://youthcard.eu/en/support). For information about personal data, read the [privacy policy](https://youthcard.eu/en/privacy-policy).
